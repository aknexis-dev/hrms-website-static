Our Story

Two Decades of Shaping the Future

Vidya Vihar International School was founded in 2005 with a simple but powerful vision: to create a school where every child is known, valued, and inspired to reach their highest potential.

From a small campus with 200 students, we have grown into one of the region's most respected educational institutions with over 2,500 students, 150+ faculty members, and a legacy of academic excellence.

We are affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and follow a curriculum that blends strong academics with co-curricular richness, character education, and 21st-century skills.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our 20-year story of growth and excellence

2005

School Founded

Vidya Vihar International School opens with 200 students and 20 teachers, offering Grade 1 to Grade 8.

2008

CBSE Affiliation Achieved

Received full CBSE affiliation, extending programs through Grade 12 with Science, Commerce, and Arts streams.

2011

New Campus Expansion

Expanded to a 10-acre campus with new laboratories, a library, sports complex, and auditorium.

2015

1000 Students Milestone

Crossed 1,000 enrolled students. Introduced Pre-Primary section and NIOS program.

2019

Smart Campus Initiative

Digitized all classrooms with smart boards, launched parent app, and introduced STEM MakerSpace.

2024

National Recognition

Ranked among Top 50 CBSE Schools in the country. Students achieved national merit in Science Olympiad.
