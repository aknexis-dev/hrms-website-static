Admission Procedure

A simple, transparent, 4-step process to join our school

1

Submit Enquiry

Fill out the online enquiry form or visit our admissions office. Our counselor will contact you within 24 hours.

2

Campus Visit

Schedule a school tour. Meet our faculty, visit facilities, and understand our educational philosophy.

3

Submit Application

Complete the admission form with required documents. An interaction session may be scheduled for some grades.

4

Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation, complete fee payment, and get your welcome kit. You're part of the family!
