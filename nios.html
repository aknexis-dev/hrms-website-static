National Institute of Open Schooling

We are an officially recognized NIOS Study Centre offering flexible, government-accredited education for students who need an alternative path to certification — whether due to health, personal circumstances, or as a second chance at board exams.

NIOS certificates are equivalent to CBSE and are accepted for higher education admissions across India.
